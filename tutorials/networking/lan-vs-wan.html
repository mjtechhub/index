LAN vs WAN

Networks are categorized by their geographic scope. The two most common types are LAN and WAN.

Networks are categorized by their geographic scope. The two most common types are LAN and WAN.

LAN (Local Area Network)

A network confined to a small area, like a single building, home, or office. It is usually fast and privately owned.

WAN (Wide Area Network)

A network that spans a large geographical area, connecting multiple LANs. The Internet is the largest WAN.

Troubleshooting Tip

If you can print to your local printer but cannot load a website, your LAN is likely fine, but your WAN connection (internet) is down.
