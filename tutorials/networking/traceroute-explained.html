Traceroute Explained

Traceroute is a utility that records the route (the specific gateway computers at each hop) through the internet between your computer and a specified destination computer.

Why Use It?

While ping tells you IF a destination is reachable, traceroute tells you WHERE the connection fails along the path.

Related Command

Use tracert (Windows) or traceroute (Linux) to map the network path.
