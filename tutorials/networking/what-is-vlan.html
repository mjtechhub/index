What Is VLAN?

A VLAN (Virtual Local Area Network) logically separates a single physical network into multiple isolated virtual networks.

Why Use VLANs?

Security and performance. For example, you can put Guest Wi-Fi on VLAN 10 and Corporate devices on VLAN 20. Even though they share the same physical switches, they cannot communicate unless routed through a firewall.
