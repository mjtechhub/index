Public vs Private IP Address

IP addresses are split into public and private ranges to conserve addresses and improve security.

IP addresses are split into public and private ranges to conserve addresses and improve security.

Private IP Address

Used inside your LAN. It is not routable on the internet. Common ranges are 192.168.x.x or 10.x.x.x.

Public IP Address

Assigned by your ISP to your router. This is the address the internet sees. Your router uses NAT (Network Address Translation) to translate between your private devices and the single public IP.
