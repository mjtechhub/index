At MJ Tech Hub, accessible from mjtechhub.github.io, the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by MJ Tech Hub and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

MJ Tech Hub is designed as a static educational resource. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not provide forms that collect your personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) directly on this site.

2. Local Storage (Cookies)

To enhance your user experience, MJ Tech Hub uses your browser's localStorage mechanism strictly to save your UI preferences. Specifically, we store:

  • Your preferred theme setting (Light Mode or Dark Mode) to persist your choice across pages and subsequent visits.

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

3. Hosting and Server Logs

MJ Tech Hub is hosted on GitHub Pages. As part of providing this hosting infrastructure, GitHub may collect standard internet log information, such as your IP address, browser type, internet service provider (ISP), date/time stamp, and referring/exit pages. This information is collected by GitHub and is governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to other websites of interest. However, once you have used these links to leave our site, you should note that we do not have any control over that other website. Therefore, we cannot be responsible for the protection and privacy of any information which you provide whilst visiting such sites and such sites are not governed by this privacy statement.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you require any more information or have any questions about our privacy policy, please feel free to reach out via the project's GitHub repository.
