DECISION BENCHMARK

Network Diagnostic Workbench

Educational troubleshooting decision tool. Input observed symptoms to systematically isolate TCP/IP fault domains without guessing.

Browser Execution & Privacy Architecture

This browser-based tool does not run commands, scan hosts, or test your network. It analyzes the observations you provide. All logic executes locally in your client sandbox to formulate fault domain hypotheses and recommended next steps.

Step 1: Enter Observed Diagnostic Symptoms

1. Can you ping your local Default Gateway?
2. Can you ping a known Public IP address (e.g. 8.8.8.8 or 1.1.1.1)?
3. Does DNS successfully resolve domain names (e.g. nslookup or ping by hostname)?
4. Can the destination TCP port be reached (e.g. Test-NetConnection or curl)?
