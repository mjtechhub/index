MJ Tech Hub was created by Mayur Talsaniya, an IT professional with hands-on experience across enterprise networking, IT infrastructure, end-user computing, system administration, virtualization, monitoring, and IT operations.

His practical experience includes enterprise firewalls, routing and switching, VLANs, Wi-Fi infrastructure, VPNs, Windows and Linux servers, Proxmox virtualization, virtual machine deployment and migrations, virtual storage management, backup and recovery planning, infrastructure monitoring, and IT service operations.

Rather than focusing only on theory, Mayur believes IT skills are best developed by understanding how technologies work, troubleshooting real problems in virtualized and physical environments, and implementing solutions practically.

MJ Tech Hub was created to turn that experience into clear, reusable learning material for IT professionals, engineers, support teams, administrators, and anyone building their technical skills.
